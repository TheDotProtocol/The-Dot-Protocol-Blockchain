3. Pricing Model

⚠ Decision Required: The pricing model below conflicts with other references in the project.

This document proposes a unified pricing model. The existing codebase references three different prices:

  • Presale preview: $0.50 per token (whitelist, $2.5M–$10M raise range)
  • ICO page: Early Bird $0.005 / Seed $0.008 / Public $0.01
  • Hexchange AMM: 3DOT/USDT pool seeded at 1:1 ($1.00)

These must be reconciled before any public-facing materials go live.

Recommended Pricing Model

ContextProposed PriceRationale
Presale (all phases)$0.005 – $0.01Low entry price creates early community. 10B–50B tokens sold at $0.01 = $100K–$500K raise.
Hexchange launch price$0.01AMM pool seeded at 100 3DOT = 1 USDT (0.01 per token). Aligns with Public sale price.
Stabilisation target$0.01 (initial) → $0.10 (growth)Stabilisation contract targets 10x over 18 months via collateral ratio adjustments.

Why NOT $0.50 or $1.00 for presale?

  • At $0.50 per token × 1T supply = $500B market cap — unrealistically high for a new L1
  • At $1.00 per token × 1T supply = $1T market cap — exceeds Bitcoin's ATH
  • The $0.005–$0.01 range gives room for 10x–100x growth and aligns with realistic L1 launch valuations
  • Presale participants receive meaningful token allocations at accessible prices
